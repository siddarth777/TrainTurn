Network Junction Replay

The trained Double DQN, unchanged from single-station training, runs the 8-platform junction inside a seven-station, double-track line with traffic in both directions. Trains start at the four line ends, call at six minor stations that follow a fixed local rule, queue for the junction, and run on to the opposite end. Switch the controller to replay the same day with a heuristic at the junction instead.

Replays were re-run in SUMO 1.28.0 with the saved checkpoint (best model from episode 135 of the 8-platform, 128-train training run). Junction and network totals match multistation/results/network7_bidir_eval3 exactly. The agent was trained on eastbound traffic only; westbound trains appear to it as unseen corridors C/D. The days are the three held-out scenarios of that preliminary evaluation (seeds 200000–200002). Positions are SUMO vehicle positions sampled every 10 simulated seconds, interpolated along each train's actual path. Drawn train length is its share of each track. Infrastructure, timetables, late starts and dwell extensions are synthetic and uncalibrated.
