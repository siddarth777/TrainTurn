Where the two DQNs come from

Validation delay during training. The untrained controller is the episode-0 network. The trained one is the best checkpoint, from episode 135. Both are the same architecture with different weights.

The days were picked by the trained DQN's margin over priority-aware across all 28 replayed days: the median day, its best and its worst. Each replay was re-run in SUMO 1.28.0 from the saved checkpoint and day file; end-of-day totals match realdata/results/ddu_sep2024_replay. Positions are SUMO vehicle positions sampled every 10 simulated seconds and interpolated in between. The outer-signal queue and upstream reservoir are boundary queues in the adapter, so queued trains are listed rather than drawn on track. Track layout is schematic. Timetable and incoming delays: RSTGCN September 2024 data (IIT Kharagpur). Platform lengths, train lengths and dwell overruns are assumptions; the data contains no freight.
